Meet Dr. Saptarshi Mahanta

A compassionate specialist committed to excellence in medical diagnostics and patient care.

Dr. Saptarshi Mahanta is a highly regarded Internal Medicine Specialist and Diabetologist based in Guwahati, Assam. With extensive experience in managing chronic conditions and acute illnesses, he focuses on providing personalized treatment plans that empower patients to lead healthier lives.

He currently consults at the prestigious Pratiksha Hospital, where he integrates modern medical evidence with a patient-centric approach.
